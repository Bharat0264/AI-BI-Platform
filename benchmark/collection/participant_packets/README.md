# Give this to participants

This is a small research exercise about whether a data-analysis system should answer, ask a clarification question, or refuse when a business question is unclear. It is **not a test of you**. You may decline participation or skip any case; tell the coordinator rather than guessing.

You will see public UCI data and a small set of task cards. Do not use AURA-BI, ChatGPT, spreadsheets shared by another participant, or another participant’s work to decide answers. Use a calculator or your own spreadsheet for arithmetic. Use only a pseudonym chosen by the coordinator; do not write your full name, email, student ID, or phone number in a form.

The coordinator sends Annotator A only the `annotator_a` packet and Annotator B only the `annotator_b` packet. Completed forms go to separate private folders. The adjudicator receives completed A/B forms only after both are locked.
