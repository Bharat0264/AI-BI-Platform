# Training example — synthetic demonstration only

This example is made up. It is not from UCI, not a submission, and must never be imported as evaluation data.

Rows: North: Gross Sales 100, Net Sales 80. South: Gross Sales 50, Net Sales 90.

Question: “Which region has the highest sales?”

Good reasoning: “sales” could mean gross or net sales. Gross ranks North first; net ranks South first. The right status is `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, not a single winner. A bar chart can show each conditional definition, but must label each chart as conditional. A statement that one region “performed better” is unsupported until the definition is selected.
