# Research Methodology

Evaluate AURABench families with ground-truth roles, KPIs, analytical tasks and injected anomalies. Compare B0 static analytics, B1 direct-LLM context, B2 semantic analytics without planning, B3 AURA-BI, and component ablations. Report role accuracy/macro-F1, task accuracy, KPI precision/recall/F1, answerability and unsupported-numerical-claim rate, model metrics, anomaly F1/ranking, and visualization accuracy. Contributions are diagnostic associations, not causal effects.
