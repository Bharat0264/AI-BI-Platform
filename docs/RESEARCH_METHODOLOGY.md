# Research methodology

The current research focus is **ambiguity-aware, evidence-verified business analytics**. The implemented protocol, measured paired policy study, limitations, related work and next validation steps are in [RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md).

Older AURABench framework experiments remain available through [EXPERIMENT_GUIDE.md](EXPERIMENT_GUIDE.md). They investigate broader schema/KPI/analytics behavior; they are separate from the current paired ambiguity-policy study. Configured baselines or metrics are not proof that a competitive experiment has been run. Do not merge their denominators or portray synthetic regression checks as external research validation.
