# Pilot recruitment and blind procedure

Recruit at least two independent participants with business-data literacy and one separate adjudicator. Provide participant information/consent and institutional review requirements before sharing data. Assign pseudonyms only; retain identity mapping outside the repository.

The coordinator distributes exactly one private role folder, a read-only UCI archive, and assigned task cards to each annotator. Annotators cannot access the other participant’s submissions, model output, hidden labels, or adjudication material. After both append-only submissions are locked, the coordinator exposes both to the adjudicator, who records disagreements and rationale. No coordinator may edit submissions; corrections are appended with a timestamp.

Pilot tasks are workflow material only. Human participants must author or substantively review the questions before they can enter any external evaluation candidate pool. No pilot count supports statistical claims.
