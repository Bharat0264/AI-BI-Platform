# AURA-BI adjudicator handbook

Adjudication begins only after two locked, independent annotation records exist. Compare records field by field, preserve every disagreement, and resolve against the frozen source snapshot and documented business context—not system output. If evidence remains insufficient, retain `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` or `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED` rather than forcing a numerical ground truth.

Use `benchmark/collection/adjudication_form.json`. Cite both annotation IDs, identify each disputed field, state the final decision and rationale, and avoid overwriting original annotation records. An adjudicator may reject a case for missing provenance, unclear licensing, unresolved unit/currency policy, or irreducible ambiguity.
